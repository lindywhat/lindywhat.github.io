## NimmaHop! Monthly Sunday Tea Dance

There are worse things to do on a lazy Sunday afternoon than going for some swing outs and having a cup of tea or two. Every month our team of Lindy Hop enthusiasts organizes a Sunday Tea Dance, sometimes with a DJ, sometimes with live music. Keep an eye on our Facebook page or on our agenda for the next Sunday Tea Dance!

## Swing SOS: Swing Social On Saturday with live music & DJ's

Every once in a while we organize an evening of social swing dancing in Nijmegen, with live music and DJ's to boot! Keep an eye on our Facebook page or on our agenda for the next Saturday party!
