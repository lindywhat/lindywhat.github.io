Since 2014 we’re the home of all Lindy Hop dancing in Nijmegen! With our own laid-back vision we try to spread the love for swing dance in and around Nijmegen, Arnhem, Kleve and where ever you are from willing to travel to our beautiful city!

Below are some highlights from the past years:

**What’s Hop!? February 2019**

This year we celebrated our fifth anniversary as a dance club. Here you can see the dance performances by our amazing guest teachers at the big Saturday night party, with live music by our dear Kalishnikovs.
