---
title: "Workshop with Simon and Frederique"
---

We welcomed Frederique from Eindhoven to give a workshop with Simon, all based upon the theme of Rhythm and Play! We worked on breaking down our Lindy Hop moves into bite-sized chunks and playing around with mixing them up. The final goal was to get everyone to play wth their footwork and body movement to express themselves in time with the music. There was a visible and audible change in everyone’s dancing: well done all for your hard work and marked improvement!
