On the occasion of our 7th birthday, international Lindy Hoppers **Ari and Simon** walked, danced and hopped us through an energetic, fun routine that they choreographed specially for us. We love it! It will take some practice, but luckily, we got a good recap from our online workshop, so when it is safe again, we will start practicing together. In the parc? On zoom? Either way, hopefully, on our 8th birthday we can meet again and show guests from other dancing scenes the Lindy What dance.
