---
title: "Lindy Hop Nijmegen Launch Party"
---

Come and celebrate with us our first night of Lindy Hop here in Nijmegen! We’re organizing a swing dance party on Friday 21st February 21.00 onwards at Café Restaurant Trianon. We’ll kick off with an introductory lesson for complete beginners with Rosalind and Simon, then we’ll dance the night away to the best of swing music from DJs Mathias and Simon, all of this for free!
