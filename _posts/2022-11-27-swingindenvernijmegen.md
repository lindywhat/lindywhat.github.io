As you know, the Lindy Hop Nijmegen roots are going back to 2014 when we started our club in this awesome city! From the very beginning, people from all over the world visited us to teach us how to swing out, like Swingin’ Denver did back in 2015. You probably know them from their extensive and top quality YouTube videos covering every aspect of swing dancing. However, you might not know that the recaps of these classic workshops were actually preserved and available for your viewing pleasure!
