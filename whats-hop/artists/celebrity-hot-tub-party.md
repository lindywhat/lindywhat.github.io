## Celebrity Hot Tub Party

![Celebrity Hot Tub Party](/assets/whats-hop/images/celebrity-hot-tub-party-1024x712.webp){: .left}

This band holds a very special place in our hearts, as it was specifically created with Nijmegen Lindy Hoppers in mind! It soon grew to be one of the known names for swing jazz both in and outside the dance community. Pumpin’ basslines, django-esque solo’s, romantic accordeon lines that sweep you off and on your feet, with some sweet harmonies to boot! All that, but with Lindy Hoppers in mind! If their rendition of Sweet sue doesn’t get you bouncing, nothing will!
