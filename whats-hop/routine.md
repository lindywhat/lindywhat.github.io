## The Whats Hop!? Routine
{: .cream}

We were honoured to have had international Lindy Hoppers Ari and Simon as teachers at our first What’s Hop!? in 2019. During the covid lockdown we kept contact with them. In that period on the occasion of our 7th birthday (2021) they choreographed a new solo routine specially for us. We loved it! We learned the routine with them in an online workshop and received the recap you see below. We use this to keep the routine alive, so we can keep performing it in Nijmegen.

In 2024 at the 10th anniversary of Lindy What!? we organised our biggest festival yet. It was the first festival after the covid years. This made it the perfect moment to showcase the routine to the community. We asked the Kalishnikovs to make an arrangement of the song *Dominic Swing* tailored to the occassion. Many attendees either learned the routine beforehand or with us during What’s Hop!?

You can be sure that learning and performing the What’s Hop!? will be part of our festival again. Come join in!
