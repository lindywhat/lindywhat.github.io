## Buy your Ticket

If you came to this page it means that you’re interested in coming to our festival. Thank you so much!

You can either go for the Full Festival Pass (workshops and parties) or only do the parties with the All Party Pass. But if you can only join for one night you can also Pick a Party. The All Party Pass and Pick a Party will only become available at some later date (follow us on social media to stay up to date).

Before you buy a ticket please read our [Terms and Conditions](/whats-hop/terms-conditions/) carefully for all information about procedures of admittance, payments and more.

Also please read our [Code of Conduct](/whats-hop/code-of-conduct/). Good behaviour should come naturally. In the Code of Conduct we specify the requirements we all have to keep in mind to make this festival a safe and happy place for everyone to dance, teach and make music to their hearts’ content. This holds not only for the festival participants, but the organisers and facilitators in the venue and city as well.

### Registration process for Full Festival Pass

This year, registration happens in **two steps**.

**Step 1 – Sign up and Group Placement**

In the first step, we only want to know what level you want to register for and we’ll ask some questions about your dancing experience. Placement depends on both available space and a balanced follow/lead ratio. If enough people register we will create a fourth group as we did the last years! If your preferred group is full or the balance isn’t right, we’ll add you to the waiting list and do our best to find a spot for you as new registrations come in.

**Step 2 – Complete your registration**

Now that you’ve secured your spot in one of our groups, it’s time to add the finishing touches to your weekend.

In this step, you can:

* **Reserve your ticket** (€10) for the Saturday theatre show (only 130 seats available!) See: [Reframing HERstory Art Foundation](/whats-hop/artists/reframing-herstory/)
* **Pre-order your lunch** for Saturday and/or Sunday See: [Lunch in De Boog](/whats-hop/artists/sporthal-de-boog/)
* **Let us know if you’d like to host or be hosted**
* **Contribute to the solidarity fund**, helping dancers who need a little extra support
* **Sign up as a volunteer** to help during the weekend

Please make sure to use the **same email address** you used in Step 1 – this helps us match details correctly.

After you submit the Step 2 form, you’ll receive a **payment request**. Your registration is **final once payment has been received**.
