## Ticket
{: .grey}

### The Full Festival Pass has a waitinglist. Why?

We want to keep the follow/lead ratio fairly even, so there might be a waiting list for some roles in some levels until more of the other role registers.

### How is my participation checked at the festival?

Owners of the Full Festival Pass register their presence at first arrival at the informationdesk. Your name and payment will be checked. After validation you will receive a bracelet with a design matching your track. Please wear this bracelet during the festival because it is your entry pass for workshops and parties.

An Informationdesk will present at the entrance of parties and workshops.

### How to signup with a partner?

Both partners have to sign up seperately and both have to fill in the name of the partner in the signup form. Only when both partners have registered they can be taken from the waitinglist for further processing.

### Can I signup without a partner?

Yes, but you will be placed on a waitinglist. As soon as there is a ticket available with a matching role/level both will be taken from the waitinglist for further processing.

### Can I buy a partyticket at the door?

No. Last year all available party tickets where sold out at the online sale. We expect that it will happen this year too (fingers crossed).

### I have to cancel my ticket. Can I get a refund?

No, but you can try to sell your ticket to someone else. Keep in mind that that person has to participate with the same role and level you signed up for. Also you have to notify the organization per email and provide the name of your replacement. By doing so we can update our administration and perform a positive check at the informationdesk.

### Do you provide discounts for people who can't afford the event?

The Lindy What!? team promotes solidarity between dancers. A solidarity fund is created based on donations. The funds collected will be used for the sole purpose of providing discounts to dancers who cannot afford to participate in the event. To apply for a discount, please email us.

The discount in 2026 only applies to the Full Festival Pass and amounts to 50%.

If you want to donate to this solidarity fund, you can indicate this in the second step of the registration with the amount of your donation. Your donation will be billed together with the Festival ticket.

## Payment Transactions
{: .grey}

### How do I pay at the information desk?

We try to minimize financial payments at the desk. Most transactions will be done beforehand (tickets by bank transfer) and checked at the desk. Payments at the desk will be done by means of a QR code facilitating payment by phone. For safety reasons we avoid cash payments.

### How do you pay in shops in Nijmegen?

Although you could expect cash payment to be possible everywhere this is not the case. It has become common practice to do daily financial transactions with a bank/credit card or phone instead of cash money. As it happens in some places you can not even pay with cash. Small shops and unattended pay terminals often only accept payment by cards or phone for safety concerns. So please come prepared and bring a bank card.

Horeca at the festival expect payment by cards or phone.

## Party Venue
{: .grey}

### Can I bring my own water for consumption?

We know that dancing makes you thirsty. It is therefore allowed to take a sip of water from your own bottle in the bag/luggage area of the party venues. However, large bottles of water are not allowed in any of the party venues at/around the dance floors. If large water bottles are placed in the dance hall, the staff of the venues may remove them, for you to pick them up at the bar later.

Of course there is a bar where you can buy a drink, so there are plenty of ways to replenish your thirst.

### Can I put chalk or powder under my shoes?

Chalk or other material for shoes to make it more slippery is not allowed in any of the venues. We will get a fine if the floor gets covered in chalk; so if we see anyone using chalk under their shoes, we will come and talk to you, and ask you to remove this from your shoes or use different shoes. We will deal with the situation accordingly and may have to note down your name in case we get a fine from the venue.

### Are there specific rules for cleaning the shoes before dancing?

Please dance in clean shoes suitable for dancing in order to protect the floor surface.

Don’t use chalk or powder on your shoes if your shoes are not slippery enough.

### Are lockers present for savekeeping my belongings?

No, there are no lockers present and the coatcheck is not guarded.
