We are a community of enthusiasts passionate about the social dance culture of the swing era of jazz, in particular the **Lindy Hop** in the beautiful city of Nijmegen (NL). We organize swing dance [classes](/lindy-hop-lessons/), [workshops](/events/) and [social dances](/agenda/) with live music. Our organization was founded in 2014 and completely consists of volunteers, based upon the tenet that Lindy Hop is a very social and enjoyable dance, where we enjoy one other's company and **have a good time dancing with everybody, no matter their skills**.
